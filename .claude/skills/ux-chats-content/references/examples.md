# The UX Chats — Few-Shot Examples

One strong + one weak example per format, with the reasoning. Read the weak example and notice it isn't *bad writing* — it's competent, generic marketing copy. That's exactly the failure mode to avoid: technically correct, zero voice.

Every strong example below is real (sent to the community as-is) or lightly adapted from a real post — trimmed for length, or with exclamation-stacking dialed back to match the writing rules. Sources are in `source-material/` (or `content-reference/source-material/` in the website repo).

---

## 1. Newsletter Intro

**✅ Strong:**
> Excuse me? Do you even know what you missed yesterday?
>
> You missed a genuinely good portfolio session (great questions, real energy). This issue: the recap, what's coming next, and a Game Night sneaking onto the calendar.

**❌ Weak:**
> Welcome to another edition of The UX Chats Newsletter! We hope this email finds you well. This month, we have exciting updates to share with our amazing community.

**Why the strong one works:** Real recap email opener, adapted. Direct address instead of a greeting, and it assumes the reader already cares enough to feel like they missed something. The weak version could be sent by any newsletter, about anything.

*Source: `luma-blasts.md` — "Umm... do you know what you missed yesterday?" (Jun 19 blast, Athira).*

## 2. Event Announcement

**✅ Strong:**
> Isn't life in UX confusing and busy enough? Let's not talk about work for once.
>
> UX Chats Game Night is back. No portfolio reviews, no design critiques, no "what's your process." Just games, chill conversation, and people who get it. The game itself is a surprise.
>
> Free. Online. Thursday, 6pm PT. luma.com/TheUXChats

**❌ Weak:**
> We're thrilled to announce our upcoming Game Night event! Join us for an evening of fun and networking with fellow designers. Don't miss out on this amazing opportunity to connect with the community. RSVP now, spots are limited!

**Why the strong one works:** Real Game Night listing, condensed. Names what it isn't (portfolio reviews, critiques) before saying what it is, and the "surprise" detail does more work than any hype line could. The weak version leads with "we're thrilled to announce" and closes with manufactured urgency that isn't backed by any real detail.

*Source: `luma-events.md` — Game Night, July 1st.*

## 3. LinkedIn Post (Event Recap)

**✅ Strong:**
> Dear Diary, Remind me never to say "Yeah! We'll just tidy a few slides real quick."
>
> Three of us turned the Herbst Theatre into a caffeine-fueled design zone last night, tweaking slides, syncing fonts, and laughing our way through the chaos as the city went quiet around us.
>
> It wasn't glamorous. It was teamwork, care, and pure fun.
>
> By the end, we did the only reasonable thing: Waymo'd home, half-delirious, fully proud.
>
> Next one's already on the calendar. Come vibe with us. ✦

**❌ Weak:**
> What an incredible event we had last night! Our team worked hard to make sure everything was perfect, and it really paid off. We're so grateful for everyone who came out to support The UX Chats. Can't wait to see you at the next one!

**Why the strong one works:** Real LinkedIn post, unedited. Specific scene ("Herbst Theatre," "caffeine-fueled," "Waymo'd home"), a register shift from funny to earnest, and a self-deprecating hook. The weak version is a string of true-but-empty sentiments that could describe literally any event.

*Source: `linkedin-posts.md` — TED AI 2025 recap.*

## 4. Guest Spotlight

**✅ Strong:**
> We're bringing a Product Design Recruiter to UX Chats. Carl is joining for a real, useful, slightly nerve-wracking session on what hiring actually looks like from the recruiter's side.
>
> Salary conversations, common application mistakes, portfolio tips that actually matter. Then Hot Seat: Recruiter Edition — a few brave volunteers answer "tell me about yourself" live, and Carl tells them what's working and what isn't.
>
> Wednesday, 6pm PT. luma.com/TheUXChats

**❌ Weak:**
> We're excited to welcome our guest speaker, a Senior Product Design Recruiter with years of experience at leading companies, to share their valuable insights this week! Don't miss this opportunity to learn from an industry expert!

**Why the strong one works:** Real Luma guest listing, dialed back from the original's exclamation-heavy draft to match the writing rules. Leads with what makes the session useful (real hiring info, live feedback) instead of a title, and "Hot Seat: Recruiter Edition" is a specific, slightly scary hook. The weak version is a resume read aloud — "years of experience," "leading companies," "industry expert" are status markers, not reasons to show up.

*Source: `luma-events.md` — Carl Wheatley, July 15th.*

## 5. Call to Action (standalone)

**✅ Strong:**
> You don't just follow design trends, you question them. You've got hot takes, hard-won lessons, and stories from the trenches. We want them for our next guest spot. Nominate yourself: theuxchats.co/become-a-guest

**❌ Weak:**
> We're always looking for passionate community members to get involved! There are so many ways to contribute to The UX Chats. Join our Discord community today to learn more about all the exciting opportunities available to you!

**Why the strong one works:** This is real production copy from the Become-a-Guest page, repurposed as a standalone CTA. One ask, one reason, one link, no throat-clearing. The weak version buries the actual action under three sentences of vague enthusiasm and never says what happens after you click.

*Source: `site-copy.md` — Become-a-Guest "You're a fit if..." section.*

## 6. Follow-Up Reminder

**✅ Strong:**
> Tomorrow, 5pm PT. Come for the portfolio. Stay for the existential design talk. luma.com/TheUXChats

**❌ Weak:**
> Just a friendly reminder that our event is coming up tomorrow! We hope you can make it. Don't forget to RSVP if you haven't already. We'd love to see you there!

**Why the strong one works:** The closing line of a real event listing, repurposed as a reminder. States the fact (time, link) and adds one specific, funny callback instead of re-selling the event. The weak version's "just a friendly reminder" and "don't forget" add words without adding information.

*Source: `luma-events.md` — Portfolio session, June 17th.*

## 7. Short Product/Update Blurb

**✅ Strong:**
> Every portfolio session now comes with a Token-Saver Skill. Same Claude, way fewer wasted tokens.

**❌ Weak:**
> We're excited to announce a brand new bonus for our portfolio sessions! We've created a helpful skill to save tokens. Check it out and let us know what you think!

**Why the strong one works:** Real bonus line from a real event listing, tightened to two sentences. States the update, then earns its place with a reason to care. The weak version pads a simple update into "we're excited to announce" filler.

*Source: `luma-events.md` — Portfolio session, June 17th ("Bonus" section).*

## 8. LinkedIn Post (Build in Public / Learning Mode)

**✅ Strong:**
> Redesigning my portfolio. Day 3. I've taken about 27 million detours.
>
> I coded it instead of using Framer, because this is my passion project. Someone pointed out my token usage is probably high with the AI agent I'm building for it. Yeah, it is.
>
> So instead of scaling back, I read everything I could find about Claude token usage. Turns out message 30 costs as much as messages 1 through 30 combined. That explains a lot.
>
> Building a skill now that teaches better token usage and catches inefficiencies early. More updates to come.

**❌ Weak:**
> Excited to share that I'm redesigning my portfolio! It's been a great learning experience diving into new AI tools and workflows. Can't wait to share the final result with you all!

**Why the strong one works:** Real LinkedIn post, condensed. Leads with a real number played for comic effect ("27 million detours"), states a real cost (token usage) as a human problem before it's a technical one, and ends with a concrete next step instead of vague excitement. The weak version could describe any project, by anyone.

*Source: `linkedin-posts.md` — Portfolio redesign / token efficiency, Day 3.*
