# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

UX designers at every stage — students breaking in, career switchers, mid-level practitioners, and senior mentors. They share one trait: they want honest, unstructured conversation with other designers, not another polished networking event.

## Product Purpose

UX Chats is a casual online UX community where designers can be **raw and real**. It exists because the dominant mode of design community — panels, personal-brand pitching, LinkedIn carousels — leaves no room for the conversations designers actually want: venting, celebrating wins, swapping losses, and learning from each other without pretense.

Success means **growing event attendance**. More people in the (virtual) room.

## Positioning

The anti-corporate design community. No panels. No pitching. No LinkedIn voice. Just designers at every stage getting together every two weeks to talk shop the way people actually talk. A neighboring product could offer the same event formats, but they could not truthfully claim the same absence of career theater.

## Operating Context

- **Events:** Every other Thursday, 6:00 PM PST, on Zoom. Two formats — free Community Nights (games, conversations, craft) and paid Special Guest Nights (mentors, psychologists, creative minds).
- **Discord:** The always-on front door. Members say hi, share work, and keep conversations going between events.
- **Luma:** Event discovery and registration lives at luma.com/TheUXChats.
- **Website:** The public face — explains what UX Chats is, shows upcoming events, introduces the hosts, and funnels visitors to Luma and Discord.

## Capabilities and Constraints

**Capabilities:**
- Landing page with hero, events, about, host bios, testimonials, FAQ, and CTAs
- Become-a-Guest page for prospective speakers
- Event cards linking to Luma registration
- Testimonial submission form
- Discord and Luma as off-site community infrastructure

**Constraints:**
- Community Nights are always free — this is a binding promise
- Special Guest Nights and workshops are paid/ticketed
- Site is deployed to GitHub Pages (static hosting only, no server-side logic)

**Terminology:** "Community Night," "Special Guest Night," "crew" (not "members"), "hosts" (not "organizers").

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** UX Chats
- **Domain:** theuxchats.co
- **Voice:** Casual, warm, a little cheeky. Second person. Short punchy lines. Emoji are on-brand as accents.
- **Core phrases:** "raw and real," "take off your tie," "no stiff intros," "Come as you are. Leave your LinkedIn voice at the door."
- **Visual identity:** Playful "sticker zine" — thick black outlines, hard offset shadows, rotated stickers, chunky display type (Bricolage Grotesque), warm cream paper. The anti-SaaS.
- **Founders:** Jacki (founder) and Romit (co-founder) are the public faces of the community.

## Evidence on Hand

- **Testimonials:** Real member quotes in `ux-chats-app/metadata/testimonials.json`
- **Photos:** Polaroid-style event photos (game night, the crew, real talk), community call screenshot, host photos
- **OG image:** Social share image at `/img/og-image.png`
- **Absences:** No attendance metrics, revenue figures, or growth data are published. Testimonials are attributed by name; do not fabricate attributions or metrics.

## Product Principles

1. **Casual over corporate.** Every decision — from copy to visuals to event format — should make the visitor feel they can show up as themselves.
2. **Community over personal brand.** The product serves the crew, not individual career positioning. Hosts are introducers, not gurus.
3. **Honesty over polish.** Real conversations, real people, real photos. No stock imagery, no sanitized testimonials, no corporate gloss.
4. **Inclusion without gatekeeping.** No experience check, no title requirement, no "must have X years." Students and seniors share the same room.
5. **Events are the product.** The website's job is to get people to an event. Everything else — Discord, testimonials, about copy — supports that funnel.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target: **WCAG 2.2 AA** conformance. The site should be usable by designers of all abilities. The community itself is explicitly inclusive of all experience levels, backgrounds, and career paths.
